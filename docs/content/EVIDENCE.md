## Rules

- A gag whose only receipt is `needs-josh` is **held** (not cut) until answered.
- When Josh confirms, replace the bracket with the fact and set status `confirmed`. Keep
  the original resume quote where there was one.
- Never merge two receipts into one bigger-sounding claim. (E-11 vs E-23 is the live example.)
- `withheld` means Josh has decided the fact does not appear on the site. Do not re-ask.
